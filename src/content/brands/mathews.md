Mathews builds a bow that rewards a good setup. Stiff riser, quiet on the shot, forgiving of a
draw that is not perfect on the fiftieth arrow of the night. What it will not do is fix a fit
that was wrong from the start, and that is where a shop with a range attached earns its keep.

## Why buy a Mathews here

We have carried Mathews for years, and the company has named us a Top 100 Dealer more than once.
That matters less than what it means day to day: we know the platform, we stock the modules, and
we have set up enough of them to know quickly whether a bow suits the person holding it.

Every Mathews that leaves this shop has been fitted to your draw length and draw weight, paper
tuned, timed, checked for center shot, and shot on our own range. Then it gets looked over a
second time. That has been the rule here since 1957 and it has not loosened.

## See it, draw it, tune it here

The showroom and the eleven-lane indoor range are in the same building. You can take a bow off
the wall, have it set to your measurements, and put arrows through it at twenty yards in the same
visit. Nothing about that is possible from a web page.

Bring the bow you shoot now if you have one. Comparing a new Mathews against your current setup
on the same night, on the same lane, tells you more than any spec sheet.

## What comes with it

Buy a bow from us and you get a discount range card, honored for a year from the purchase date.
Come back and practice on it. Bring it in when it needs a look. The relationship does not end at
the register, which is most of the point of buying from a family shop rather than a warehouse.
