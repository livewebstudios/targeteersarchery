Hoyt has been building bows longer than most of the people shooting them have been alive, and it
shows in the details you only notice after a season: the way a riser holds still, the way a cam
system keeps its timing, the way a target rig stays where you put it.

## Why buy a Hoyt here

Hoyt has named us a Top 50 Dealer multiple years running. What that reflects is volume and
service, and the service half is the one you feel. We stock the parts, we know the cam systems,
and we have tuned enough Hoyts to spot a problem before it becomes a bad group.

Target archers and hunters want different things out of the same brand. Tell us which one you
are, honestly, and the conversation gets a lot shorter.

## See it, draw it, tune it here

Take one off the wall, let us set it to your draw, and shoot it on the range in the back. Eleven
lanes, air conditioned, out to twenty-seven yards. If it does not feel right in your hand at full
draw, no spec sheet is going to talk you into it, and we are not going to try.

## After the sale

Fitted, paper tuned, timed, center shot checked, shot on our range, then looked over a second
time. Any bow bought here comes with a discount range card good for a year, so you have somewhere
to put the practice in.
