Most hunts end early for the same reason. Not the deer, not the wind. You got cold, or you got
wet, and the last hour of legal light went by from the truck. SITKA is built around fixing that
specific problem, and it does.

## Why SITKA is worth the money

It is a system, not a jacket. A base layer that moves moisture instead of holding it, a mid layer
that does the actual insulating, and an outer layer matched to the weather you are sitting in.
Buy pieces at random and you get an expensive pile of clothes. Buy the system for how and where
you hunt and you get quiet, dry and still.

New Jersey and the northeast throw a particular mix at you: damp cold rather than dry cold, big
temperature swings between the walk in and the sit, and a lot of stand hunting where you are not
generating any heat of your own. That combination punishes cotton and rewards layering.

## Fitted in person

Draw a bow in the jacket before you buy it. A hunting layer that binds at full draw is worse than
no layer at all, and you will not discover that from a size chart. Come in, put it on, and pull
your own bow in it.
