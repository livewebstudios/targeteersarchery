There is no cart on this website. That is a choice, and people ask about it, so here is the
reasoning.

## A bow is not a size

Buy boots online and the worst case is you send them back. A bow is not like that.

A bow that fits you is a set of numbers: draw length measured off your actual wingspan and anchor,
a draw weight you can hold at the end of a long sit rather than haul back once in a parking lot,
a grip that puts your hand in the same place every time, a rest and a release matched to how you
shoot.

Get those wrong and you do not end up with a bow that is slightly less good. You end up with a bow
that teaches you bad habits, because you will unconsciously contort to make it work. Undoing that
takes a year.

## The box does not come tuned

A bow shipped to your door has been assembled, not tuned. Nobody has paper tuned it, checked
center shot, set nock height, timed the cams to your draw, or tied in a peep at your anchor.

None of that is optional. It is the difference between a bow that shoots and a bow that shoots
where you are looking. And it requires a bench and a range, which is precisely what you are opting
out of when you order online.

## What you actually save

Less than the sticker suggests, once you pay somebody to set it up. And you give up the part that
does not show on any invoice: somebody who knows your bow.

When your groups open up in March, you call a shop that has your numbers on file. When your
broadheads print six inches off in September, you drive fifteen minutes. When you need a part on a
Thursday before opening weekend, there is a person who will look for it.

## What we are not saying

We are not saying you are a fool for buying online, and we are not going to make you feel like one
at the counter.

**Bring us the bow.** We tune bows that were bought somewhere else all the time and we do it
without commentary. That is a service, not a favour, and you are as welcome here as anybody.

## What buying here gets you

Fitted to your measurements. Paper tuned, timed, center shot checked, nock height set. Shot on our
range before it leaves. Then looked over a second time, which is a rule that predates everybody
currently working here.

And a discount range card, honored for a year from the day you buy, so there is somewhere to put
the practice in.

## The larger point

This shop has been here since 1957 because people came back. Not because of a transaction. Because of
what happened after it.

You cannot ship that. So we did not build a store that pretends you can.
